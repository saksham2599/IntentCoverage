# Community conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Focus criticism on code and ideas. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sharing private information, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

Use issues and pull requests for project work; avoid spam and unrelated promotion. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect contributors. Enforcement should be proportionate and consider the context.

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/saksham2599). Do not post another person's private information publicly. For a security vulnerability, follow SECURITY.md instead.
